# 25% research protocol

The target is a saleable rise of at least 25% from a prior completed one-minute low to a later high within a conservative 480-minute rolling window.

Controls must use the same symbol and comparable historical timing and are rejected where a 25% crossing contaminates the applicable window. Predictor data must end before each decision timestamp. Discovery, validation and sealed-test outputs must remain separated.

No trading rule is included in this release. Rules discovered from the 50% population must not be assumed to generalise to the 25% population.
